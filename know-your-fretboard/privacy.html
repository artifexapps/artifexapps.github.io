Know your fretboard Privacy Policy

Leer en español

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This policy explains what data the Know your fretboard app (the "App"), developed by Artifex Apps ("we"), uses on Android and iOS, and why.

In short: we don't ask you to sign up, we have no servers of our own that receive your data, and we don't sell personal information. Your progress is stored on your device and, if you turn it on, in your Google Play Games or Game Center account. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID depending on your consent.

1. Data the App stores on your device

The App stores, only on your device:

  • Your progress: completed challenges and levels, picks, tutorials watched, daily challenge.
  • Your preferences: guitar type, sound and other settings.
  • A record of purchases already delivered (so they aren't delivered twice) and whether you removed ads.

This data is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data in the system settings.

2. Cloud backup (Google Play Games / Game Center)

If you sign in to Google Play Games (Android) or Game Center (iOS), the App saves a copy of your progress in that account's "saved games", so you can restore it or move it to another device. The copy includes the progress data from section 1 and the device name, so you can tell your sessions apart.

That information is stored by Google or Apple in your account, under their own policies:

  • Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

We have no access to your account or your sign-in details. You can use the App without signing in; you just won't have a cloud backup.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To serve and measure them, AdMob may collect data such as the device's advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system, language and ad interaction data.

  • Where the law requires it (for example, in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some US states), the App asks for your consent with Google's form before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice in Settings → Ad privacy options.
  • On iOS, the App may ask for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency). If you don't grant it, ads aren't personalized with the advertising ID.
  • You can reset or turn off the advertising ID in your device settings.
  • If you buy Remove ads, the App stops requesting ads.

More information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543.

4. In-app purchases

Purchases (pick packs and remove ads) are processed by Google Play or the App Store. We don't receive or store your payment details: we only receive the purchase confirmation and its identifier, to deliver what you bought.

5. How we use data

  • To run the App and save your progress.
  • To restore your progress across devices, if you use the cloud backup.
  • To show ads in the free version and deliver purchases.

We don't use the data to build profiles of our own and we don't sell it to third parties.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your rights

At any time you can:

  • Delete local data by uninstalling the App or clearing its data.
  • Delete the cloud backup from the Google Play Games or Game Center / iCloud settings.
  • Change your ad consent in the App, and the advertising ID on your device.

Under applicable law (for example, Argentina's Personal Data Protection Law 25,326 or the EU GDPR), you can also request access to, correction or deletion of your data by contacting us. Argentina's Agency for Access to Public Information, the supervisory authority under Law 25,326, handles complaints regarding breaches of personal data protection rules.

8. Security

Data is stored on your device and in Google's or Apple's services, which apply their own security measures. No system is completely secure, but we don't send your data to servers of our own.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will publish the new version at this same address, with its update date. If the changes are significant, we will announce them in the App.
