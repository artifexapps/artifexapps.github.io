Artifex Apps Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This policy applies to all the apps published by Artifex Apps ("we") and to this website. Each app also has its own privacy policy with the details of what it does; if they differ, the app's policy prevails.

In short: we don't ask you to sign up, we have no servers of our own that receive your data, and we don't sell personal information. What our apps store stays on your device, unless you use a service of Google or Apple that the app offers, or you share something yourself.

1. Our apps

App What it uses Detailed policy
Know Your Fretboard Data on the device, Google Play Games backup (optional), Google AdMob ads, Google Play purchases Privacy Policy
Easy Live Looping Microphone, data on the device, sharing through Android, Google Play donations Privacy Policy

2. Data stored on your device

Our apps store your progress, your creations (for example, recordings) and your settings on your device. That data is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in the system settings. If you turned on your device's backup to your Google or Apple account, the system may include the app's data in that backup, under their terms.

3. Permissions

Our apps ask only for the permissions they need for their features, and explain why in their own policy. For example, Easy Live Looping uses the microphone only to record and to calibrate latency, never in the background.

4. Services of Google and Apple

Depending on the app, these services may process data under their own policies:

  • Google Play Games / Game Center: if you sign in, a copy of your progress is saved in your account so you can restore it on another device.
  • Google AdMob: apps with ads may use the device's advertising ID, IP address and device and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads. Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, and you can change it from the app.
  • Google Play / App Store purchases: payments are processed by the store. We don't receive or store your payment details, only the confirmation of the purchase.

More information: https://policies.google.com/privacy, https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

5. Sharing

When an app lets you share something (for example, an audio clip), the file goes only to the app or person you choose. We don't receive a copy.

6. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics or forms. GitHub may record technical data such as your IP address when you visit it, under its own policy: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement.

7. Children

Our apps are not directed to children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

You can delete the data of our apps by deleting it from the app, clearing its data or uninstalling it, and manage your Google or Apple account data from their settings. Under applicable law (for example, Argentina's Personal Data Protection Law 25,326 or the EU GDPR), you can also request access to, correction or deletion of your data by contacting us. Argentina's Agency for Access to Public Information, the supervisory authority under Law 25,326, handles complaints regarding breaches of personal data protection rules.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will publish the new version at this same address, with its update date.
