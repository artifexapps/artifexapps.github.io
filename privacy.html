Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Artifex Apps ("we", "us") handles information in our mobile apps and games, including Know Your Fretboard (the "App"). Artifex Apps is operated by Alejandro David Castro, an independent developer based in Argentina.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account with us, and we do not collect your name, email address or other personal information on our own servers. The App stores your progress, settings and scores locally on your device.

The App uses the following third-party services provided by Google, which may collect information as described in their own policies:

  • Google Play Games Services — if you sign in, Google provides the App with your Play Games player ID and display name to enable achievements and leaderboards. Your scores and achievements are stored by Google.
  • Google AdMob — used to show ads. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, approximate location and ad interaction data in order to deliver, measure and, where permitted, personalize ads.
  • Google Play Billing — in-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details; we only receive confirmation that a purchase was completed.

2. How information is used

  • To provide the App's features, such as saving progress, achievements and leaderboards.
  • To deliver in-app purchases.
  • To show advertising that helps keep the App free.
  • To understand crashes and improve the App.

3. Advertising and your choices

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and can change their choice at any time from the App. On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings under Google → Ads.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the Google services listed above, as needed for them to work. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and in how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

5. Data retention and deletion

Data stored locally is removed when you uninstall the App. Play Games data is managed by Google and can be deleted from your Google Play Games profile settings. You may also contact us to ask questions about your data.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us.

7. Security

We rely on the security measures of Google's services and of your device's operating system. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable care to protect information.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to object to certain processing. In Argentina, these rights are recognized by Law 25,326 on Personal Data Protection. To exercise them, contact us at the address below.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
