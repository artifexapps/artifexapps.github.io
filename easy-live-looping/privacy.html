Easy Live Looping Privacy Policy

Leer en español

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This policy explains what data the Easy Live Looping app (the "App"), developed by Artifex Apps ("we"), uses on Android, and why.

In short: your recordings never leave your phone unless you share them yourself. The App has no ads, no analytics, no accounts and no servers of our own, and we don't sell personal information.

1. Microphone

The App asks for permission to use the microphone, and uses it only:

  • To record the clips and tracks of your loops, while you are recording.
  • To measure the latency of your audio route when you calibrate it (the App plays a test sound or a click and listens for it or for your claps).

The App doesn't listen in the background and doesn't send audio anywhere. If you deny the permission, you can still use the App, but you can't record or use the automatic calibration.

2. Data the App stores on your device

The App stores, only on your device:

  • Your loops and songs: recorded audio, keyboard and drum clips, harmonic bases, names, meters, tempos and edits.
  • Your latency calibration profiles and your settings (language, theme, metronome and others).
  • Which donations you made, so the App can show the heart in the header.

This data is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data in the system settings. If you turned on Android's backup to your Google account, Android may include the App's data in that backup, under Google's terms; you can manage it in your device settings.

3. Sharing

When you share a clip or an instrument, the App creates the file (audio or MIDI) on your device and opens Android's share sheet. The file goes only to the app or person you choose, under that app's own policy. We don't receive a copy.

4. Donations

Donations are one-time purchases processed by Google Play. We don't receive or store your payment details: we only receive the confirmation of the purchase from Google Play, to show your heart. Google's privacy policy applies to the payment: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

5. What the App doesn't do

  • It doesn't show ads or use an advertising ID.
  • It doesn't use analytics, crash reporting or tracking services of its own or from third parties.
  • It doesn't ask you to create an account and doesn't access your contacts, location, photos or files other than its own.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect personal data from anyone, children included.

7. Your rights

All the App's data stays on your device, under your control: you can delete loops from the App, or everything by clearing its data or uninstalling it. Under applicable law (for example, Argentina's Personal Data Protection Law 25,326 or the EU GDPR), you can also contact us with any question or request about your data. Argentina's Agency for Access to Public Information, the supervisory authority under Law 25,326, handles complaints regarding breaches of personal data protection rules.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will publish the new version at this same address, with its update date.
